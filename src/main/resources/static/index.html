How It Works

Three simple steps to manage classroom learning content.

1

Teacher Uploads

Teachers upload videos, PDFs, images or other educational content.

2

Grant Permission

The teacher selects the classrooms that should have access to the content.

3

Classroom Views

Students in the classroom can access the content that has been permitted.
